## PART 1 — Review requests (the #1 ranking move)

**First, get your review link:** in your Google Business Profile dashboard →
"Ask for reviews" / "Get more reviews" → copy the short link (looks like
`g.page/r/...`). Paste it wherever you see `[REVIEW LINK]` below.

**Golden rule:** ask within 24 hours of finishing a job, while they're happy.
A text gets 5x the response of an email. Never offer anything in exchange for a
review — that's against Google's rules and can get your profile pulled.

### Text #1 — send right after the job
> Hey [First Name], thanks again for having Blackridge out — glad you're happy
> with how it turned out. If you've got 30 seconds, a quick Google review really
> helps a small local shop like mine get found. Means a lot: [REVIEW LINK]
> — Michael

### Text #2 — gentle nudge, 3 days later if no review
> Hey [First Name], no pressure at all — just circling back in case you meant to
> leave that review and it slipped. Here's the link again, takes a minute:
> [REVIEW LINK]. Appreciate you either way. — Michael

### Email version — if you only have their email
> Subject: Quick favor?
>
> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thanks again for trusting Blackridge Builders with your [project]. It was a
> pleasure working with you.
>
> If you were happy with the work, would you mind leaving a quick Google review?
> It's the single biggest thing that helps folks around [town] find us. Takes
> about a minute: [REVIEW LINK]
>
> Thanks a lot,
> Michael West
> Blackridge Builders · (304) 405-2106

### In person / leave-behind line
> "If we did right by you, the best thanks is a quick Google review — I'll text
> you the link before I head out."

### How to answer reviews (do this — Google rewards it)
- **5-star:** "Thanks [Name] — glad you're happy with the [project]. Call anytime."
- **Critical:** stay calm, own it, offer to make it right offline. Never argue.
  "Sorry it fell short, [Name]. That's not how we do business — I'll call you
  today to make it right. — Michael"
