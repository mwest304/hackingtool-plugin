# First-Reply Templates — Blackridge Builders

Starting points for the instant first reply. Chip personalizes each to what the
lead actually wrote — never sends a template cold. All under 100 words. Swap
‹brackets› for real detail; `{{slot}}` = a real open time pulled from Calendar.
Keep the final signature from `../../config/voice-profile.md`.

> These are drafts. Nothing sends until the owner approves.

---

> quick look in person — no charge. I've got {{slot A}} or {{slot B}} open this
> week. Which works and I'll lock it in? If neither, tell me a couple windows and
